How do i change my password?

To change your password:


  1. click the settings icon in the top left on the dashboard.
  2. Navagate to 'account security'
  3. Follow the prompts to change your password.

How to delete my acount!

To delete your account:


  1. click the settings icon in the top left on the dashboard.
  2. Navagate to 'account security'
  3. Click 'delete acounnt'
  4. if you are sure you want to delete your acounnt. enter your password.
  5. click 'confirm deletion'
